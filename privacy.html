Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit the wedding website of Bhakti and Rohit.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that you voluntarily provide when using our site's interactive features via Google Login:

  • Basic Profile Information: Your name and profile picture (used to personalize your experience and messages).
  • Email Address: Used strictly for identifying your contributions to the guestbook and jukebox.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely for wedding-related celebrations:

  • Personalizing the digital invitation letter addressed to you.
  • Displaying your name and photo alongside well-wishes you post.
  • Managing the collaborative Spotify Jukebox and Photo Gallery.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your messages and participation data are stored in a private Google Spreadsheet accessible only to the couple. We do not store your Google or Spotify passwords. Authentication is handled entirely by the respective service providers.

4. Third-Party Links

Our site includes links to Google Photos and Spotify. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external platforms.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We will retain your well-wishes as a digital memento of our wedding. However, if you would like your data or messages deleted, please contact Rohit or Bhakti directly, and we will remove your information immediately.

6. Contact Us

For any questions regarding this privacy policy, please reach out to the couple via the contact details provided in your formal invitation.
